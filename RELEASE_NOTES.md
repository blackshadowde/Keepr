**Bujji, your typing buddy**
- Bujji floats above the text box, follows your words and watches what you type. It's squishy: touch it and it wobbles, drag it and it stretches after your finger, let go and it springs back
- It has a life of its own: hops onto a new photo to look, peeks out from behind your messages, runs over to someone's typing dots, and sleeps on the newest message at night. Start typing and it hops straight back
- It brings the right thing: a hammer when you pin a message, a broom when you delete, a screwdriver when you edit, an umbrella when rain comes up, a party popper for birthdays, coffee in the morning, headphones while a voice note plays, and it catches your heart reactions
- Moods: hugs a heart when someone sends love, winks at a hello, goes shy when you call it cute, puts its glasses on when it answers, gets dizzy when you shake the phone, wears a crown on a streak, and turns its back if you poke it too much (hold it to make up)
- @bujji remembers notes, birthdays and reminders, kept only on your phone (no AI). It offers a reminder when a time comes up in a chat
- @bujji knows Settings: "@bujji open notification settings", "@bujji turn on dark mode", "@bujji search typing indicators in settings"
- It tells you about live locations and check-ins: when someone starts sharing, is close by, arrives, or misses a check-in
- Settings > Typing buddy: Lively, Calm (stays on the text box) or Off

**Location**
- A new map: smooth pinch, turn and 3D tilt, sharp street names at every zoom, light or dark with the app
- One sheet to send a spot (your picture is the pin), share live for 15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours, check in, or send only roughly where you are
- In the chat, a card with the sender's picture as the pin and how far it is. Open it for Google Maps, Waze, directions or the coordinates
- A live map of everyone sharing in a chat, with the time each has left, the way they're heading and their trail
- Check-ins have their own screen, a bar on the home screen shows when you're sharing, and the notification has Stop and +30 min

**Chats**
- Bubbles curve round the sender's picture, and a new message grows out of their typing dots
- Hold a message and, without lifting, slide onto an action or a reaction; let go to pick it
- Polls, View once messages, and threads one tap away beside the chat's name
- Invites show who invited you, with Accept and Decline
- Stickers and pictures load with a soft shimmer

**Home**
- Your profile on top, Chats and Spaces with their unread counts, and unread chats as cards you can answer right there

**Security**
- Post-quantum direct chats between Keepr users: an extra layer of ML-KEM-768 (libcrux, formally verified) inside the normal encryption
- Strict encryption: a fresh key for every message
- Sign in through your server's own page; your sign-in and encryption keys are kept in the phone's secure storage

**Calls**
- Native calls (beta, off by default): Settings > Chat > Native calls

**And more**
- matrix: links open in Keepr, custom emoji in reactions, a verified badge in the chat header, 1117 new translations, and many fixes
