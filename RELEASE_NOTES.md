**Messages**
- Replies: the message you're answering peeks out from behind the top of your reply, as a smaller card in that person's own colour, with their picture (or the photo itself), their name and the first words. Tap it to go to the message
- Select text: hold a message and pick Select to choose just some words, then Copy them (line breaks and all) or Quote them in a reply. The message's numbers are listed underneath, one tap to copy
- Codes and phone numbers in messages are faintly underlined: tap a code to copy it, tap a phone number to call it or copy it
- Messages written without a connection wait and send themselves once you're back online, for up to two days, instead of failing after a few seconds. Messages still waiting when Keepr was closed are sent when it's online again
- Once a message is pinned, its button in the hold menu shows the pin with a line through it, for Unpin
- Messages scroll on behind Bujji's row down to the text box instead of stopping above it

**Wallpapers**
- Keepr's own chat wallpapers: Doodles, Flow, Shapes, Waves and Dots, all painted from your theme's colours, so they match every theme, light and dark
- A Pattern slider sets how strongly the drawings show
- Your own photo is softened to fit: a little blur, tinted with your theme, with a Dim slider for how far it fades back
- Music: the cover of the song you're playing (from your Last.fm or ListenBrainz connection) fills the chat's background, soft and calm, and changes with the song. Streamed songs get their cover too, and with nothing playing your photo shows (or the plain background)
- The wallpaper now reaches the bottom edge, behind the navigation bar, and stays still when the keyboard opens
- All in Customisation > Chat wallpaper

**Sounds**
- Sound packs: Glass, Wood, Air and Classic, in Notifications > Sounds, each with a play button. A pack covers calls, notifications and two new message sounds
- A short sound as your message goes, and one when a message comes into the chat you have open (not in muted chats). Each has its own switch, and they play along with your music instead of pausing it

**Finding people**
- Type just a name, like rahul, to find someone on Keepr's server; a full @name:server works as before
- People on Keepr's server show as @rahul; people on other servers keep their server in the name
- Your profile's code, Share and Copy link now give a short link, get-keepr.github.io/u/yourname, and Keepr opens these links straight into a chat

**Bujji**
- When someone reacts to a message of yours that's scrolled away, Bujji holds the emoji up and hops now and then; tap Bujji to go to the message. Lots at once come in a small basket, tipped out above the text box, and Bujji is worn out for a moment
- With Bujji off, the reaction floats just above the send button instead

**Profile and pictures**
- A square crop opens after you take or choose a profile picture, or a chat's or group's picture: move and resize the square, turn it, then Done. Moving pictures stay moving
- Your animated profile picture plays on your own profile page
- The clear-time choices under your status sit side by side again

**Smoother**
- Calmer motion everywhere: lists, panes, buttons and the hold menu settle softly instead of bouncing, and pages slide a little calmer
- The chat wallpaper is drawn once and reused, so scrolling and Bujji moving stay light
- When the bar under the messages grows or shrinks (the voice recorder, more lines), the messages glide instead of jumping
- Voice notes: the bars of your voice glide in on every frame, and the recorder rises in softly
- Keepr asks your phone for its highest refresh rate
- Dragging a slider no longer swipes the page back
- For checking smoothness: tap the version in About five times for a frame timing graph and Smooth touch

**And more**
- Block screenshots, in Security & sign-in (Android): screenshots and screen recordings of Keepr come out blank, and so does its preview in recent apps
- Photos no longer show twice when one had to be sent again after a busy server or a dropped connection
- "Sorry" writes itself across the screen in one soft, glowing handwritten stroke
- Nothing shows behind Bujji's row above the text box any more
- The iPhone share sheet says Keepr
