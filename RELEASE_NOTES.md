**Messages in cards**
- A person's messages in a row share one card, as wide as the widest, with a hairline between them; their picture sits beside the newest with the card curving round it, and the time and ticks sit inside
- Typing looks the same: their picture with a small card and three dots in their colour, joining their card when they wrote last
- A reply's line runs down the card to the sender's picture
- Jump to present is a small round button right above the send button

**Links**
- A link's preview sits inside the message's card, and the link itself is left out of the text once the preview shows
- Hold a link for Open link, Copy link and Share

**Voice notes**
- Recording opens a pane in your colour theme: the time with a breathing dot, your voice as bars that rise and fall, and Bujji listening in its headphones, with big Cancel, pause and Send buttons
- In the chat a voice note is a card like a text: a round play button that glows while it plays, bars that fill as it plays, the length and a speed chip

**Photos**
- The photo grid no longer flickers; picked photos show their number and are sent in that order
- A new All photos tile opens the phone's own photo picker, Google Photos too
- Holding a photo in a stack opens the same menu as any message, and reactions show under the stack

**Home**
- One quiet line at the top: your picture, the page's name, search and new chat, with room above it that folds away as you scroll
- Tap "Chats" for a switch to Spaces (hold it for locked chats)
- Bujji sits on the name holding up how many chats are unread

**Your profile**
- One page for everything: your picture, name and colours, your ID, your status with 1 hour, Today, This week or Don't clear, your Keepr ID code with Share and Copy link, the name pill, group tag and settings

**What you're listening to**
- Connect ListenBrainz on your profile with just your username, no password and no notification access: a Listening now card on your profile and headphones with the song next to your name
- Spotify, YouTube Music and most players can feed it; Last.fm follows in an update

**Bujji**
- Listens along to your music: headphones on, a gentle sway and the odd hop onto your messages; a sad song makes it sad
- Poke it too much and it hides behind a message until you find it and pet it
- No background behind its row above the text box any more

**Deleting a message**
- The samurai moves with weight: drops in, cuts the message in one dash, and without looking back drives his sword into the ground behind him and walks away

**Panes**
- Swiping a pane down follows your finger and closes at the speed of your flick
