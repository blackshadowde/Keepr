**What's new, told by Bujji**
- After this update Bujji says hi and walks you through what's new, channel by channel, like a chat: it types first, then posts each change with a live mockup
- "Show me where" opens the real screen, dims the rest and Bujji points at the spot
- It ends with confetti and every part ticked. Ask "@bujji what's new" to watch it again

**Bujji keeps the tool in hand until the job is done**
- A pen from when you start editing a message until the edit is sent (or cancelled)
- A letter while you reply, a magnifier while you search the chat
- The hammer, broom, camera or paper until a pin, delete, photo or file has gone through, then a proud face as it puts it down

**Threads**

- In a thread, Rename and Close are right in the top bar, and every thread can be closed, also ones started in other apps
